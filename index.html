Map Preview

      

        The preview is created from a temporary WGS84 GeoJSON conversion and shown over satellite imagery.
        The DXF must use the correct real-world coordinate system to appear in the right location.
      


      
Important

      

        A CAD file drawn around local coordinates such as 0,0 without georeferencing cannot be correctly
        positioned on satellite imagery. Survey CAD using actual UTM coordinates will align correctly.
